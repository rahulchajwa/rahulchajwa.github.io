      <h3>
        Towards a Physics of the Living Ocean  
      </h3>

      <p class="article-meta">
        Living Futures · Invited Essay · 2024
      </p>

      <p>
        Imagine the ocean not as a static reservoir, but as a living circuit in motion, where microbial life, 
        fluid flows, and chemical gradients intertwine to produce rich dynamical patterns. A single perturbation, 
        such as seeding iron-rich dust or alkaline sediments into the ocean, ripples through trophic layers, 
        reshapes microbial consortia, and shifts the ocean’s state in ways that currently defy predictions. 
        To capture these dynamics and trace how perturbations propagate across scales, especially in the context of a changing climate, the language of physics is indispensable.
