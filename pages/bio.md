I am a physicist interested in self-organization, adaptation, learning, and emergence in soft condensed matter and biological systems, with a focus on suspension dynamics in the oceans and their role in climate regulation. My cross-disciplinary research combines theory and experiments to study non-equilibrium dynamics across scales — from microhydrodynamics of sedimentation to ecosystem fluxes via marine snow. Some of these investigations are conducted in field settings, to capture the full richness and complexity of natural processes.
