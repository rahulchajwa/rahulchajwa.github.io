### Soft Matter & Biological Physics in Field Setting
Table-top experiments have played a central role in advancing soft-matter physics. Extending these investigations into field environments, although challenging, holds immense potential for uncovering new mechanisms in nature and for achieving a more holistic understanding of physical dynamics in their native context. With the changing climate, I believe this approach will become increasingly essential. Guided by this perspective, during my postdoctoral work in the [Prakash Lab](https://prakashlab.stanford.edu), I have been involved in conducting table-top experiments aboard research vessels to probe microscale processes that sequester carbon in the oceans.
